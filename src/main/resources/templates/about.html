Tournament formats

Different formats. One competitive platform.

Checkspire supports several tournament structures so players and organizers can choose how they want an event to be played.

♞

Swiss Tournaments

Compete over multiple rounds against players with comparable tournament scores without requiring every participant to face everyone.

♜

Round Robin

Every player faces the other participants, creating a complete competition where consistency across all games matters.

♛

Single Elimination

Advance through a knockout bracket where every match matters and the final remaining player wins the tournament.

2

Competitive chess

More than tournament brackets

Checkspire combines tournament management with the tools needed to play competitive online chess.

Ratings & Time Controls

Players maintain separate Bullet, Blitz, Rapid and Classical ratings. Games and tournaments can use multiple time controls and can be Rated or Casual.

Live Games & Spectating

Games run in real time with live chess clocks, move history and automatic timeout handling. Tournament games can also be followed while they are being played.

Tournament Standings

Follow tournament rounds, boards, player scores, standings and match results throughout an event.

Direct Challenges

Players can challenge each other directly, choose a time control and color preference, and play Rated or Casual games.
