About Checkspire

Competitive online chess built around tournaments.

Checkspire is an online chess platform focused on competitive play, live games and tournament competition. Create or join events, compete across multiple tournament formats and build separate ratings across different chess time controls.

Competitive chess

More than tournament brackets

Ratings & Time Controls

Players maintain separate Bullet, Blitz, Rapid and Classical ratings. Games and tournaments can be Rated or Casual.

Live Games & Spectating

Games run in real time with chess clocks, move history and automatic timeout handling. Tournament games can also be spectated live.

Tournament Standings

Follow rounds, boards, player scores, standings and match results.

Direct Challenges

Challenge another player, choose a time control and color preference, and play Rated or Casual games.
